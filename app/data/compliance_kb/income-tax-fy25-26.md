# Indian Income Tax Rules & Slabs — FY 2025–26 (AY 2026–27)

## 1. Overview of Income Tax Regimes

In India, salaried employees can choose between two tax regimes for each financial year:
1. **New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC):** Default tax regime with lower slab rates and higher tax-free threshold, but disallowing most Chapter VI-A itemized exemptions/deductions (except standard deduction, employer NPS under 80CCD(2), and employer PF contribution).
2. **Old Tax Regime:** Traditional tax regime with higher slab rates, but allowing all Chapter VI-A deductions (80C, 80D, 80CCD(1B), 80E, 80G), Section 10 exemptions (HRA, LTA), and Section 24(b) home loan interest.

---

## 2. New Tax Regime Slabs (FY 2025–26 / AY 2026–27)

Under Union Budget revisions effective for FY 2025–26:

| Total Taxable Income Slab (₹) | Income Tax Rate |
| :--- | :--- |
| Up to ₹3,00,000 | Nil (0%) |
| ₹3,00,001 to ₹7,00,000 | 5% |
| ₹7,00,001 to ₹10,00,000 | 10% |
| ₹10,00,001 to ₹12,00,000 | 15% |
| ₹12,00,001 to ₹15,00,000 | 20% |
| Above ₹15,00,000 | 30% |

### Key New Regime Benefits:
- **Standard Deduction:** Increased to **₹75,000** for salaried employees and pensioners.
- **Section 87A Tax Rebate:** Tax rebate is available for taxable income up to **₹7,00,000**. Tax rebate amount is up to ₹25,000, meaning tax payable becomes ₹0.
- **Effective Tax-Free Salary:** With the ₹75,000 standard deduction + ₹7,00,000 rebate threshold, any employee with gross annual salary up to **₹7,75,000 pays ZERO income tax**.
- **Family Pension Deduction:** Increased from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000.

## 3. Old Tax Regime Slabs (FY 2025–26 / AY 2026–27)

For individuals below 60 years of age:

| Total Taxable Income Slab (₹) | Income Tax Rate |
| :--- | :--- |
| Up to ₹2,50,000 | Nil (0%) |
| ₹2,50,001 to ₹5,00,000 | 5% |
| ₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000 | 20% |
| Above ₹10,00,000 | 30% |

- **Senior Citizens (60 to 79 years):** Basic exemption is ₹3,00,000.
- **Super Senior Citizens (80+ years):** Basic exemption is ₹5,00,000.
- **Standard Deduction:** Fixed at **₹50,000**.
- **Section 87A Rebate:** Applicable only if total taxable income is up to **₹5,00,000** (max rebate ₹12,500).

---

## 4. Health & Education Cess and Surcharge

- **Health and Education Cess:** **4%** applied on the sum of income tax and surcharge.
- **Surcharge on High Net Worth Individuals:**
  - Income ₹50 Lakhs to ₹1 Crore: **10%**
  - Income ₹1 Crore to ₹2 Crores: **15%**
  - Income ₹2 Crores to ₹5 Crores: **25%**
  - Under New Tax Regime, the maximum surcharge rate is capped at **25%** (whereas Old Regime had a 37% slab for > ₹5 Cr).

---

## 5. Which Regime Should an Employee Choose?

- **Breakeven Point:** For an annual salary of ₹12,00,000 to ₹15,00,000, if an employee's total eligible deductions (80C + 80D + HRA + Home Loan Interest) exceed **₹3,75,000 to ₹4,25,000**, the Old Regime is generally beneficial.
- If total deductions are less than ₹3,50,000, the **New Regime** yields higher take-home pay with lower tax liability and no requirement to lock funds in tax-saving instruments.
