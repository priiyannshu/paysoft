# Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948 (ESIC)

## 1. Statutory Wage Threshold & Applicability

- **Applicability:** Factories and commercial establishments employing 10 or more persons in notified areas.
- **Gross Monthly Wage Ceiling:** **₹21,000 per month**.
- **Employees with Disabilities:** Gross wage ceiling is **₹25,000 per month**.
- **Wage definition for ESI:** Basic, DA, HRA, City Compensatory Allowance, Special Allowances, and attendance bonus (excludes annual bonus, gratuity, and retrenchment compensation).

---

## 2. Contribution Rates

Effective from revised Ministry of Labour rates:

| Contributor | Statutory Rate (%) | Calculation Base |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Employee Contribution (IP)** | **0.75%** | Gross Monthly Wages |
| **Employer Contribution** | **3.25%** | Gross Monthly Wages |
| **Total Contribution** | **4.00%** | Deposited with ESIC |

- **Exempt Employees:** Employees whose daily average wage is up to ₹176 are exempt from paying employee's share, but employer must pay full 3.25%.

---

## 3. Statutory Benefits for Insured Persons (IP)

- **Medical Benefit:** Full medical care for self and dependent family members from day one of insurable employment.
- **Sickness Benefit:** Cash benefit at 70% of average daily wage for up to 91 days in two consecutive benefit periods.
- **Maternity Benefit:** 100% of average daily wage for 26 weeks for confinement and 6 weeks for miscarriage.
- **Disablement Benefit:** 90% of wage for temporary or permanent disablement arising out of employment injury.
- **Dependents' Benefit:** Monthly pension to dependents in case of employment injury resulting in death.

---

## 4. Compliance Deadlines & Records

- **Monthly Contribution Filing:** Payment and return must be filed online via ESIC Portal on or before the **15th of the following month**.
- **Contribution Periods:**
  - 1st April to 30th September (corresponding cash benefit period: 1st January to 30th June).
  - 1st October to 31st March (corresponding cash benefit period: 1st July to 31st December).
