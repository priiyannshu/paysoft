# Organization Leave Policy, Gratuity & Statutory FAQs

## 1. Annual Leave Entitlement (FY 2025–26)

Every confirmed staff member is entitled to the following leave quotas per financial year:

1. **Casual Leave (CL):**
   - **Quota:** 12 days per year (credited 1 day at the start of each month).
   - **Lapse Policy:** Cannot be carried forward to next financial year; lapses on March 31st.
   - **Maximum Continuous Leave:** 3 days at a time without prior approval.

2. **Sick / Medical Leave (SL):**
   - **Quota:** 10 days per year on full pay.
   - **Medical Certificate:** Required from a Registered Medical Practitioner for illness exceeding 2 consecutive working days.

3. **Earned / Privilege Leave (EL):**
   - **Quota:** 20 days per year (accrued at 1.66 days per month of active service).
   - **Carry-over & Encashment:** Can be accumulated up to a maximum of **300 days**.
   - **Encashment at Retirement/Separation:** Tax-exempt up to **₹25,00,000** under Section 10(10AA) for non-government employees.

## 2. Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972

### Eligibility:
An employee is eligible for statutory gratuity upon completion of at least **5 continuous years of service** with the organization (or upon death/disablement without minimum tenure).

### Calculation Formula:
$$\text{Gratuity Amount} = \frac{15 \times \text{Last Drawn (Basic + DA)} \times \text{Years of Service}}{26}$$

- **Working Days in Month:** Fixed by law at **26 days**.
- **Fraction of Year:** If months in the final fraction exceed 6 months, it is rounded up to 1 full completed year.
- **Maximum Tax-Exempt Gratuity:** Up to **₹20,00,000** under Section 10(10) of the Income Tax Act.

---

## 3. Loss of Pay (LOP) & Arrears Computation

- **Daily Wage Rate:** Computed as $\frac{\text{Monthly Gross Earnings}}{\text{Days in Month}}$.
- **LOP Deduction:** $\text{Daily Rate} \times \text{Unpaid Days}$.
- **Salary Arrears:** Calculated when DA revision or pay increment is implemented with retrospective effective dates.
