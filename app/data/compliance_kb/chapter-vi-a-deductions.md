# Chapter VI-A Deductions & Section 10 Exemptions (Income Tax Act)

## 1. Section 80C — Investment & Savings Deductions
- **Maximum Limit:** **₹1,50,000** per financial year across all eligible instruments.
- **Eligible Investments & Expenses:**
  - Employee Provident Fund (EPF) 12% deduction and Voluntary Provident Fund (VPF).
  - Public Provident Fund (PPF) — 15 year government backed scheme.
  - Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS) mutual funds (3-year lock-in).
  - Life Insurance Premiums (for self, spouse, and children).
  - National Savings Certificate (NSC), 5-Year Bank Fixed Deposits (tax-saver).
  - Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) for girl child.
  - Principal repayment on housing loan for purchase/construction of residential house property.
  - Tuition fees paid for full-time education of up to 2 children in Indian schools/universities.
  - Stamp duty and registration charges paid during house property purchase.

---

## 2. Section 80D — Health & Medical Insurance Premiums
- **Self, Spouse, and Dependent Children:**
  - Regular individuals: Up to **₹25,000**.
  - If self or spouse is a Senior Citizen (60+ years): Up to **₹50,000**.
- **Parents:**
  - Non-senior parents: Additional up to **₹25,000**.
  - Senior citizen parents (60+ years): Additional up to **₹50,000**.
- **Preventive Health Checkup:** Up to **₹5,000** within the overall limit.
- **Maximum Combined Deduction:** Up to **₹1,00,000** (when both self/spouse and parents are senior citizens).

---

## 3. Section 24(b) — Home Loan Interest Deduction
- **Self-Occupied Property:** Deduction of interest paid on housing loan up to **₹2,00,000** per financial year.
- **Let-Out Property:** Full interest deductible against rental income, but loss under house property that can be set off against salary is capped at **₹2,00,000**.

---

## 4. Section 80CCD(1B) — National Pension Scheme (NPS)
- **Additional Deduction:** Up to **₹50,000** for voluntary employee contributions to NPS Tier-1 account.
- This is **over and above** the ₹1,50,000 limit under Section 80C, allowing an aggregate ₹2,00,000 deduction.

---

## 5. Section 10(13A) — House Rent Allowance (HRA) Exemption
Exempt portion of HRA is the **least of the following three amounts**:
1. **Actual HRA received** from employer during the period.
2. **Rent paid minus 10% of Salary** (Salary = Basic Pay + Dearness Allowance).
3. **50% of Salary** for accommodation situated in Metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, Chennai) OR **40% of Salary** for Non-Metro cities (Pune, Bangalore, Hyderabad, etc.).

### Form 12BB Proof Requirements:
- If annual rent paid exceeds **₹1,00,000**, employee must mandatorily furnish **Landlord PAN**.
- Rent receipts and rental agreement must be submitted for HR verification.

---

## 6. Section 80E & Section 80G
- **Section 80E:** Full interest paid on loan taken for higher education of self, spouse, or children (deductible for up to 8 continuous assessment years).
- **Section 80G:** Donations to specified charitable funds and relief institutions (50% or 100% deduction depending on qualifying fund status).
