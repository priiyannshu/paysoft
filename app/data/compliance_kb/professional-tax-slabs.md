# State-wise Professional Tax (PTax) Slabs in India

Under Article 276(2) of the Indian Constitution, the maximum Professional Tax that can be levied on any person is capped at **₹2,500 per year**.

---

## 1. Maharashtra (State Code: MH)

Under the Maharashtra State Tax on Professions, Trades, Callings and Employments Act, 1975 (with latest gender-neutral / women exemption amendments):

### Male Employees:
| Monthly Gross Salary (₹) | Monthly PTax (₹) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Up to ₹7,500 | Nil (₹0) |
| ₹7,501 to ₹10,000 | ₹175 per month |
| Above ₹10,000 | **₹200 per month** (₹300 in February) |

### Female Employees:
| Monthly Gross Salary (₹) | Monthly PTax (₹) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Up to ₹25,000 | **Nil (Exempt)** |
| Above ₹25,000 | **₹200 per month** (₹300 in February) |

*Note: Total annual tax = 11 months × ₹200 + 1 month (Feb) × ₹300 = **₹2,500**.*

---

## 2. Karnataka (State Code: KA)

Under Karnataka Tax on Professions, Trades, Callings and Employments Act, 1976:

| Monthly Gross Salary (₹) | Monthly PTax (₹) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Up to ₹24,999 | Nil (₹0) |
| ₹25,000 and above | **₹200 per month** (₹2,400/year) |

---

## 3. West Bengal (State Code: WB)

| Monthly Gross Salary (₹) | Monthly PTax (₹) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Up to ₹10,000 | Nil |
| ₹10,001 to ₹15,000 | ₹110 |
| ₹15,001 to ₹20,000 | ₹130 |
| ₹20,001 to ₹40,000 | ₹150 |
| Above ₹40,000 | **₹200 per month** |

---

## 4. Tamil Nadu (State Code: TN)

Levied half-yearly (every 6 months) by local municipal corporations:

| Half-Yearly Gross Income (₹) | Half-Yearly PTax (₹) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Up to ₹21,000 | Nil |
| ₹21,001 to ₹30,000 | ₹135 |
| ₹30,001 to ₹45,000 | ₹315 |
| ₹45,001 to ₹60,000 | ₹690 |
| ₹60,001 to ₹75,000 | ₹1,025 |
| Above ₹75,000 | **₹1,250 per half-year** (₹2,500/year) |

---

## 5. Other States & Exemptions
- **Delhi, Haryana, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh:** No Professional Tax is currently levied.
- **Telangana / Andhra Pradesh:** Monthly slabs from ₹150 to ₹200 (for salary > ₹20,000).
- **Gujarat:** Gross up to ₹12,000: Nil; > ₹12,000: ₹200/month.
